01 — CONTEXT

One supply chain, many questions

Every stage of the chain records its own data, and every team asks its own question. How much is in the plant warehouse? How is loading going at the jetty? Where is that ship? Does this kiosk still have fertilizer?

The data existed, but it was spread across systems and stages. My job was to bring it together into dashboards for each team and for the executives above them.

02 — HOW EACH DASHBOARD IS MADE

From a question to a dashboard

Every dashboard goes through the same eight steps. Most of them happen before anything appears on screen.

  1. 1

    Understand the need

    Find out what the user wants to see and what decision it should help them make.

  2. 2

    Study the raw data

    Learn what data already exists, where it lives and what shape it’s in.

  3. 3

    Design a mockup

    Sketch the visual first, so everyone agrees on the picture before anything is built.

  4. 4

    Prepare the data

    Profile, clean and reconcile it, so the numbers agree across every source.

  5. 5

    Build queries & APIs

    Write the logic that turns raw data into the exact numbers the visual needs.

  6. 6

    Build the visual

    Turn the agreed mockup into a working dashboard.

  7. 7

    Test every number

    Check the figures against their sources. One missing digit can mean a ton of fertilizer that isn’t there.

  8. 8

    Review with the user

    Walk through it together, adjust, and only then call it done.
